# Joey's Reading Room

A personal archive of the books I've read: ratings, reviews, and reading history, explored as shelves, statistics, a connection map, and a yearly yearbook. Live site: https://joeyweast.github.io/reading/

The library comes from my Goodreads export, so the ratings, shelves and reviews are my own. It covers what I've finished, what I'm reading now, what's waiting on my to-read shelf, and the few books I didn't finish.

## What's on the site

- **Home** – the book I'm reading now (or a note that nothing's on the nightstand), the latest finished books, and the collection at a glance: books read, authors, pages, and an estimate of listening hours.
- **Shelves** – every book, shelf by shelf. Search by title, author, series or review text, filter by genre or shelf, and sort by recently finished, title, author or rating. Open any book for its cover, series, rating, genre, finish date and my review if I wrote one.
- **Statistics** – the collection in numbers: a reader profile, books finished each year, the ratings I give, genres, publication eras, and most-read authors.
- **Universe** – an interactive map of the collection. Books are linked by author and series, with optional genre lines. Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom, search for a book, or pick one and explore its connections.
- **Yearbook** – a year in reading: month by month, what filled the shelf, the authors and series that kept coming back, and what the data says about the year. There are also shareable graphics in portrait, story and square formats.
- **Settings** – comfort options such as reduced motion. They're remembered on your device only.

## About

The listening-time figure is an estimate worked out from page counts, not a tracked number. Some older books have no finish date, so they appear on the shelves but not in the year-by-year views.

It's a static site on GitHub Pages and works on phones and desktops. Motion can be turned off, and the Universe can be searched and used from the keyboard. Visits are counted anonymously with [GoatCounter](https://www.goatcounter.com/).

Book covers and descriptions belong to their respective publishers and authors.
